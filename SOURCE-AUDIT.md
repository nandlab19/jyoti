## Visual and technical choice

The homepage uses a typographic black/gold opening, early concept work, connected service rows, one deliberate light process chapter, founder-led context, useful FAQs and a project CTA. Original HTML/CSS previews are used in place of an unrelated stock hero image. No competitor artwork is reused.

Fourteen crawlable pages are generated from shared templates and JSON. Small vanilla-JavaScript interactions provide filters, dashboard aggregation, gallery zoom and the local brief builder. Self-hosted Inter eliminates external font calls at runtime. No analytics, account system, CMS, learning platform or paid subscription is introduced.

All three initial portfolio entries are concepts generated for this build. They are neither historical NAND Lab engagements nor commercial proof. An unpublished needs-verification entry is supplied for future real work.

The original website and its WordPress installation have not been modified. Sites registration alone does not mean deployment. Production publication remains subject to the owner's explicit approval in the adopted master specification.
